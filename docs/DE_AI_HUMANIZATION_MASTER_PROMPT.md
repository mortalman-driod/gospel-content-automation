## 1. Executive Mandate & The "Human Touch" Philosophy

### The Problem
Automated content engines consistently suffer from recognizable failure modes:
1. **Developer-Facing UI in Client Emails:** Monospace `<textarea>` form fields, raw AI prompt dumps, debug verification logs, and pipeline quota counters that scream "automated bot."
2. **Template Monotony:** Every flyer follows the exact same mechanical 6-part vertical stack, identical font sizes, and identical yellow pill badges.
3. **Synthetic Image Plasticity:** Backgrounds possess an unmistakable "AI sheen"—hyper-smooth HDR gradients, plastic lighting, and zero photographic film grain.
4. **Copywriting Purple Prose:** Formulaic openers (*"My friend, there are seasons when..."*), repetitive clichés (*clarion call, spiritual lethargy, cease striving, intimate sanctuary*), and mechanical, forced injection of cultural phrases (*"per time"*, *"till date"*).
5. **Bot-Like Social Formatting:** Staccato bullet points followed by rocket (`🚀`), fire, and globe (`🔥🌍`) emoji clusters.

### The Standard
Every output of this system must pass the **Blind Agency Test**: If a veteran creative director, pastor, or audience member reviews the email briefing, reads the reflection, or views the flyers on Instagram, they must perceive it as the handcrafted work of an elite editorial team and master graphic designer.

## 4. Layer 2: Flyer Art Direction & Compositor (`src/design/`)

### 4.1 The 4 Rotating Human Design Archetypes
Human designers never produce the exact same layout every day. The engine must support 4 distinct layout styles, automatically selected by sermon theme:

#### Style 1: The Asymmetric Modern Split (`asymmetric_split`)
* **Usage:** Action-oriented, bold declarations, faith, overcoming.
* **Layout:** Typography strictly bounded to the left 60% of the canvas; physical subject (cross, mountain, open Bible) unhindered on the right.
* **Typography:** Bold Condensed Grotesque (`Montserrat-Black`) + Electric Yellow accent with curved acrylic brush underline.
* **Scripture:** Solid sunshine pill badge with dark navy text.

#### Style 2: The Heritage Editorial Serif (`heritage_serif`)
* **Usage:** Contemplative, stillness, worship, sovereignty of God.
* **Layout:** Centered, dignified editorial layout with generous negative space.
* **Typography:** High-contrast luxury serif (`PlayfairDisplay-Bold`) for the entire quote, with subtle italicized keyword accent.
* **Scripture:** Clean, unboxed typographic footnote framed by delicate fine divider rules (`#D4AF37` at 0.5px thickness). No bulky plastic badges.

#### Style 3: The Heavy Impact Statement (`heavy_impact`)
* **Usage:** Urgent prophetic exhortation, radical obedience, breaking limits.
* **Layout:** Giant 3-to-4 line headline taking up 65% of the upper frame. Zero subline clutter.
* **Typography:** Massive scale (up to 120px on 9:16) in high-contrast stark white with dual-tone accent.
* **Scripture:** Minimalist corner badge with high contrast.

#### Style 4: The Minimalist Quiet Light (`quiet_light`)
* **Usage:** Comfort, grief, peace, personal devotion.
* **Layout:** Understated, spacious layout positioned over deep ambient background.
* **Typography:** Medium-weight sans (`Montserrat-Bold` at 64px) with subtle tracking and generous line height.
* **Scripture:** Soft off-white text badge with subtle dark translucent backing.

### 4.2 Natural 35mm Film Grain Injection Filter
To eliminate the smooth, synthetic "plastic sheen" of AI-generated or digital canvases:
* Implement an in-memory procedural film-grain synthesis function in Pillow:
  $$\text{Pixel}_{\text{final}} = \text{Pixel} + \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2) \quad \text{where } \sigma \in [6, 10]$$
* Apply a 3% to 4% Gaussian monochrome noise layer over the composited background before rendering text.
* This introduces tactile camera sensor texture, making digital art look like authentic 35mm film stock.

### 4.3 Typography Pairing & Font Engine Expansion
* Integrate genuine TrueType font pairs:
  * Display Serif: `PlayfairDisplay-Bold.ttf`
  * Display Heavy Sans: `Montserrat-Black.ttf`
  * Editorial Subline: `Montserrat-Bold.ttf`
* Implement dynamic tracking (letter spacing) for mastheads and badges to achieve editorial refinement.

## 5. Layer 3: Anti-ChatGPT Writing Engine (`src/write.py`)

### 5.1 The 50+ Banned AI Clichés Blacklist
The writing prompt and post-generation validator must strictly forbid the following terms and structures:

```
[BANNED ABSTRACT BUZZWORDS]
clarion call, spiritual lethargy, lingering in shadows, tapestry, beacon, delve,
testament, unlock, in today's fast-paced world, embark, harness, intimate sanctuary,
gentle sovereign, inner storm, cease striving, myriad, juxtaposition, pivotal,
cornerstone, unleash, elevate, profound reminder, resonant, multifaceted, nuanced.

[BANNED STORY OPENERS]
"My friend, ..."
"There are seasons when..."
"In a world where..."
"Have you ever stopped to consider..."
"As believers, we often..."
"Sitting at 4 a.m. with cold wood palms on my kitchen counter..."
```

### 5.2 The 6 Authentic Human Story Entry Points
Every daily reflection must open with one of 6 human narrative structures:

1. **The Concrete Encounter:** An encounter with a real person, dialogue, or situation (*"A brother phoned me on Tuesday, his voice worn thin by four straight months of unanswered job applications..."*).
2. **The Piercing Paradox:** A sharp, counter-intuitive truth stated simply (*"Most spiritual burnout is not caused by doing too much for God; it comes from doing things God never asked us to carry."*).
3. **The Visceral Daily Friction:** Grounded in everyday physical realities (*"There is a unique exhaustion that hits right around 6:00 p.m. in gridlocked traffic, when your phone buzzes with yet another urgent demand..."*).
4. **The Dramatic Biblical Moment:** Cinema-grade biblical scene painting (*"Peter’s boots were soaked with salt water before his mind even registered what his feet were doing..."*).
5. **The Honest pastoral Confession:** Raw, vulnerable, authentic reflection (*"For years, I treated prayer like an emergency alarm rather than an oxygen supply..."*).
6. **The Unvarnished Observation:** Sharp cultural or human insight (*"We live in a culture obsessed with visibility, but God has always done His heaviest work in dark soil."*).

### 5.3 Cultural Cadence (Authentic West African & Global Rhythm)
* **Never** command the AI to mechanically paste phrases like `"per time"` or `"till date"`.
* **Instead**, instruct the voice:  
  *“Write with the quiet gravitas, warmth, and spiritual authority of an experienced, deeply prayerful Nigerian pastor. Use natural, unforced, dignified English with rhythmic balance, relatable cadences, and deep warmth.”*

### 5.4 Social Caption De-AI Rules
* **No Rocket/Fire/Globe Clichés:** Eliminate `🚀`, `🔥🌍`, `✨` bot formatting.
* Allow only 0 to 2 natural, understated emojis per post (e.g., a quiet prayer hand `🙏` or Bible book `📖`), used only where a human writer would naturally place them.
* Write captions as genuine letters to friends, not marketing pitches.

## 6. Layer 4: The "De-AI Smell Test" Verification Gate (`src/verify.py`)

Implement an automated pre-flight verification gate in the pipeline before any email is sent or flyer is saved:

```python
class DeAIVerificationGate:
    """
    Automated pre-flight heuristic scanner that calculates the 'AI Likelihood Score'
    of written text and layout balance. Rejects any draft that exceeds tolerance.
    """
    def verify_issue(self, package: IssuePackage) -> Tuple[bool, List[str]]:
        violations = []
        
        # 1. Banned Cliché Scanner
        for word in BANNED_AI_WORDS:
            if re.search(r'\b' + re.escape(word) + r'\b', package.reflection, re.IGNORECASE):
                violations.append(f"AI Cliché Detected: '{word}' in reflection")
                
        # 2. Formulaic Opener Check
        if package.reflection.strip().startswith(("My friend", "In a world", "There are seasons")):
            violations.append("Formulaic AI Opener Detected")
            
        # 3. Sentence Length Uniformity (Burstiness Check)
        sentences = [s.strip() for s in re.split(r'[.!?]+', package.reflection) if s.strip()]
        if len(sentences) >= 4:
            lengths = [len(s.split()) for s in sentences]
            std_dev = statistics.stdev(lengths)
            if std_dev < 3.2: # Too uniform = robotic
                violations.append(f"Low Sentence Burstiness (StdDev: {std_dev:.2f}) - Text feels robotic")
                
        # 4. Social Bot Emoji Clusters
        if re.search(r'[🚀🔥🌍]{2,}', package.facebook_post):
            violations.append("Social Bot Emoji Cluster Detected in Facebook Post")
            
        return len(violations) == 0, violations
```

If the gate detects violations, the pipeline automatically requests a rewrite using a different narrative seed, guaranteeing 0% AI detection.
